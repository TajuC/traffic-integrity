# Changelog

## 1.1.0

Layered detection: versioned signals, consistency analysis, graph correlation,
campaign baselines, optional logistic model, outcome labels, evidence bundles,
shadow policy evaluation, and a synthetic evaluation harness.

The origin still cannot prevent Google from billing a click. Stealth browsers
and unique residential IPs remain expected gaps. Evaluation numbers are
synthetic. See docs/evaluation-results.md.

## 1.0.0

Initial public release.

The Express guard, Redis observation store, conversion pipeline, reference
site, test suite, and operations documents ship together as a standalone
library. Enforcement is progressive: allow, monitor, challenge, restrict,
then block. Conversions leave the origin only after server-side qualification.
The click Google already billed is out of scope, by design.
